Who we are

Chasing Sun Africa is a Nairobi-based agribusiness working at the intersection of agriculture, technology and market intelligence. Our farm sits between Mount Longonot and Lake Naivasha, where controlled-environment greenhouses let us grow to specification, year round, for professional buyers.

“We believe that organic farming is a positive philosophy.”

Mission and vision

Our mission is to connect African growers to global demand without losing sight of where the food came from. Every crop we trade is traceable to a vetted farm, checked against the buyer's specification, and dispatched on our own logistics.

We build the business on four pillars — horticulture, commodity trading, livestock and agri-tech — combining earthy farming wisdom with precision agriculture, drip irrigation, integrated pest management and demand-led crop planning.
