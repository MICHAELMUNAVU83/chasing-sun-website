## Who We Are (`#about`)

### Who we are
Chasing Sun is an agrienterprise working at the intersection of agriculture, technology and market intelligence. Our farm sits between Mount Longonot and Lake Naivasha, where controlled-environment greenhouses let us grow to specification, year round, for professional buyers.

**Headline:** Grow with Chasing Sun.

### Outgrower (`#outgrower`)
We work with landowners to transform their land into productive, profitable farms. We bring dedicated management, expert agronomy, ready buyers for the produce, and technology that lets you monitor your farm live.

## AUM Options (`#aum-options`)

- **Headline:** Your land or capital. Our growing expertise.
- **Intro:** Chasing Sun brings land, growers and markets together through a professionally managed farming system. Choose a managed greenhouse in Kedong, put your own arable land to work, or invest in the company behind the operation.

### AUM Investment in Kedong (`#aum-kedong`)

You contribute capital. Chasing Sun provides the land, greenhouse and full operating system in Kedong, managing a cucumber and capsicum rotation on your behalf.

- Your investment supports production, with payouts that reflect the farm's actual harvest revenues.
- **CTA:** Enquire about Kedong → email enquiry.

### AUM Landowner Partnership (`#aum-landowner`)

Transform your arable land into a professionally managed farm while retaining ownership. Chasing Sun provides technology, agronomy, management and market access.

- We assess soil, water availability and market proximity, then prepare a crop programme and operating plan for your parcel.
- **CTA:** Request a land assessment → email enquiry.

### Equity Investment in Chasing Sun (`#aum-equity`)

Contribute growth capital at company level in exchange for an equity stake in Chasing Sun. Your returns reflect the growth, performance and valuation of Chasing Sun and its wider AUM portfolio.

- **CTA:** Discuss equity investment → email enquiry.

### One operating system, from planting to market

- **Technology:** A shared dashboard brings planting schedules, harvest reporting, forecasts, weather conditions and advisory into view.
- **Agronomy:** Agronomic visits, soil testing and sensor readings guide the crop programme and soil correction plan for each parcel.
- **Management:** A dedicated farm manager coordinates the crop calendar, input procurement, quality and day-to-day operations.
- **Offtake:** Our market relationships connect produce with buyers. Where possible, produce is contracted before harvest.

### Start with a conversation

1. Share your investment interests or parcel details for a land assessment.
2. Review the proposed operating plan, costs and projected economics with our team.
3. Agree the partnership terms and onboarding plan before operations begin.
