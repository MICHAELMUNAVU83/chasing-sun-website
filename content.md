## Who We Are (`#about`)

### Who we are
Chasing Sun is an agrienterprise working at the intersection of agriculture, technology and market intelligence. Our farm sits between Mount Longonot and Lake Naivasha, where controlled-environment greenhouses let us grow to specification, year round, for professional buyers.

### Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** A dependable agricultural future defined by quality and integrity.
- **Mission:** To eradicate food insecurity in Africa and beyond.
